# Q2.11 prediction (written before training B, C, D)
B (symmetry augmentation) should reduce C_M, C_R and E_R, most under rotation where the gap was largest, because the mirror/rotation weakness looked mostly like digit appearance, but it should not help at 2x or 3x. C (speed augmentation) should largely fix 2x (~6.6 px/frame, inside the 8 px reach, so most likely unfamiliar motion), but at 3x (~10 px/frame, beyond the reach) it should still be clearly worse than at 1x, and it should not improve consistency under M or R. D (larger reach plus speed augmentation) should be the only model that handles 3x well at about the same 1x accuracy, and none of A-D should be exactly consistent under M or R.
